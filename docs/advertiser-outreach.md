# Advertiser outreach

Use a small, relevant prospect list to test the sponsorship offer before
increasing volume. Start with local racket-sport shops, coaches, sports
physiotherapists, tournaments, and booking services. Record each contact's
public source, the purpose stated for that address, the date contacted, and
any objection. Keep a suppression list and do not contact an address again
after an objection.

## Email checklist

- For Netherlands business prospects, use an electronic address that is
  explicitly published for unsolicited commercial messages and use it only
  for that purpose. A public address by itself is not necessarily permission
  for a sales email.
- Do not use purchased or scraped personal-address lists. Named work contacts
  may also be personal data; apply GDPR transparency and objection rules.
- Identify yourself and the business, include a valid postal address, and
  make it easy to object at no cost.
- Keep claims factual. Listing counts are not audience counts; don't promise
  impressions, leads, or bookings that have not been measured.
- Send a relevant, individualized message. One polite follow-up is a sales
  choice, not a legal entitlement; stop if the recipient objects.
- Check the destination country's rules before contacting businesses outside
  the Netherlands.

The Netherlands-specific rule is in Article 11.7 of the
[Telecommunicatiewet](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0009950/).
GDPR rights for direct marketing are described in Article 21 of the
[GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj).
This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Pilot email

**Subject:** Amsterdam Court Finder sponsorship pilot for [category]

Hi [name],

I run Amsterdam Court Finder, which helps players compare local venues and
booking options. I'm testing one clearly labelled sponsor placement for
[relevant product/service] alongside [specific sport or location] listings.

Would you be open to discussing a 30-day pilot at a fixed fee? I can share
measured traffic once enough data is available; I won't promise bookings or
unmeasured reach.

If this is not relevant, reply “no” and I will not contact you again.

Best,

[your name]
Amsterdam Court Finder
[valid postal address]
[contact email]
